6. Addition

Method: Operate on flags and bodies independently. Apply Flag Promotion or Place Promotion when needed.

Clean case: 6 + 7

6 = 5F 1B
7 = 5F 2B
──────────
Flags: 5F + 5F = 10F
Bodies: 1B + 2B = 3B
Result: 10F 3B = 13 ✓

With Flag Promotion: 9 + 4

9 = 5F 4B
4 = 0F 4B
──────────
Bodies: 4B + 4B = 8B → Flag Promote → 5F + 3B
Flags: 5F + 0F + 5F (from promotion) = 10F
Result: 10F 3B = 13 ✓

With Place Promotion: 15 + 7

15 = 15F 0B
7 = 5F 2B
───────────
Bodies: 0B + 2B = 2B
Flags: 15F + 5F = 20F → Place Promote → 1B in twenties, 0F in units
Result: 0F1B (twenties), 0F2B (units) = 22 ✓ 
