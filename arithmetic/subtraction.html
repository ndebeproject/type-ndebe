7. Subtraction

Method: Operate on flags and bodies independently. Apply Flag Demotion or Place Demotion when needed. (Use §4 to confirm the first number is the larger one.)

Clean case: 13 − 7

13 = 10F 3B
7 = 5F 2B
───────────
Flags: 10F − 5F = 5F
Bodies: 3B − 2B = 1B
Result: 5F 1B = 6 ✓

With Flag Demotion: 13 − 9

13 = 10F 3B
9 = 5F 4B
───────────
Bodies: 3B − 4B → impossible → Flag Demotion
10F demotes to 5F, body gains 5: now 5F 8B
Flags: 5F − 5F = 0F
Bodies: 8B − 4B = 4B
Result: 0F 4B = 4 ✓

With Place Demotion: [2, 0] − [1, 10]

0F2B, 0F0B − 0F1B, 10F0B (40 − 30)   
Units: 0F0B − 10F0B → impossible → Place Demotion 
Take 1 from the twenties (2B → 1B); units receives 15F0B + 5F0B 
15F + 5F − 10F = 10F → units: 10F 0B
Twenties: 1B − 1B = 0B
Result: 10F 0B = 10 ✓
