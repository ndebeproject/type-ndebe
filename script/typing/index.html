On the website, hold a numeral for 10–19 or hold Period for marker choices. With a key focused, ArrowDown opens its choices.

Math, Currency, Teaching, and Symbols and spacing have separate labelled buttons. Hardware users can reach them with Tab, then Enter or Space.

Quotation direction and saved text

The website infers quotation direction from the text before the caret. Keyman checks up to 64 preceding characters; Option/Alt+apostrophe inserts a closing single quote or flips an adjacent one. Add Shift for double quotes.

RTF retains font formatting. TXT retains only characters: choose a Ńdẹ́bẹ́ font again if reopened plain text shows missing-character boxes. Private-use assignments are local conventions, not Unicode allocations.

Support characters and missing text

The rectangular Nsibidi design is the missing-glyph indicator. The circular design represents the replacement character U+FFFD. The dotted rectangle U+25CC provides a teaching stem placeholder. Nonbreaking space and narrow nonbreaking space keep adjacent text together without a visible mark.
